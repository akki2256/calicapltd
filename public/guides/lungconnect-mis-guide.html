Built for people who serve patients

Lung Connect India MIS is designed for patient navigators and programme managers — not for IT specialists. Everyone works in clear language, with screens that match how care actually happens.

For field & clinic teams

Navigators & operators

Search a patient in seconds, enrol someone new step by step, and capture follow-up support — guidance, financial help, accommodation, ration, and thoracic care details when needed.

For programme leaders

Admins & managers

Open a dashboard that shows enrolments, follow-ups, funding patterns, geography, and diagnosis mix. Manage who can access the system — without chasing files across email and WhatsApp.

A typical day in the system

No training manual required to understand the flow. Here is how the two main roles move through their work.

Operator journey

Stay close to every patient

  1. 1Find an existing patient by name, case number, or phone.
  2. 2Review their history, then add today’s follow-up — guidance, ration, outreach, thoracic updates if relevant.
  3. 3Enrol a new patient through short, clear sections: personal, medical, support, and remarks.
  4. 4Browse the activity list when you need the full picture of recent enrolments and follow-ups.

Admin journey

Lead with a clear view

  1. 1Open the dashboard to see enrolments, follow-ups, funding, and geographic reach at a glance.
  2. 2Click into any chart to understand the detail behind the numbers.
  3. 3Keep master lists tidy — diagnoses, hospitals, guidance options — as the programme evolves.
  4. 4Add or deactivate users so the right people have the right access.

What the product covers

Everything below is everyday language for the work your teams already do — now captured in a system that stays consistent across cities and navigators.

New patient enrolment

Guided sections for identity, contact, hospital, diagnosis, treatment planned, accommodation, guidance, outreach, and ration.

Follow-up visits

Record what support was given after enrolment, with the patient’s full context still in view.

Thoracic details & tests

Status, study enrolment, planned medicine, and per-test sample status, report date, and result.

Financial & social support

Track funding pathways, trust support, amounts received, and ration line items with quantities.

India-wide geography

Country, state, and district selection that keeps location reporting accurate for programme review.

Searchable activity data

Filter and sort enrolments and follow-ups so teams can answer questions without exporting to Excel first.

Live reporting dashboard

Visual summaries for leaders — including state, DMG, diagnosis, gender, funding, and navigator workload.

Reference & user management

Admins maintain dropdown values and user accounts so the system grows with your programme.
