Section 1

System Overview

Calicon CRM is a complete loan broking management system that helps your team track every customer from first contact to final loan settlement.

The Big Picture: A Customer's Journey
👤 New CustomerContact created
➔
📋 Deal OpensLoan opportunity
➔
📝 DocumentsCollected & verified
➔
🏦 ApplicationSent to lender
➔
✅ SettlementLoan disbursed

The system manages the entire lifecycle, including customer information, financial profiles, deal progress, lender applications, team follow-ups, communications, document signing, performance incentives, and management reporting.

📊 For the Business

  • Track every deal in a visual pipeline
  • Automated follow-up reminders
  • Performance dashboards and reports
  • Team incentive calculations
  • Complete audit trail of all actions

💼 For the Team

  • Clear to-do lists and notifications
  • One-click customer communication
  • Electronic document signing
  • Personal performance tracking
  • Easy deal handoffs between team members

User Roles & Access

Calicon CRM can be organised around as many roles as you need. The four below are one sample setup, from the people on the phones to the person who runs the system.

Agent

  • Works directly with customers

  • Can view the Sales page

💼 Sample

  • Everything a Lead can do

  • Full access in this example

  • Configures stages, lenders, and templates

Contacts (Customers)

Every person your business works with is a Contact. The contact record holds their full identity, financial profile, and links to all their deals.

In this example, a new contact's source starts as Floor. Team leads, managers, and admins can change it, and agents leave it as set. If you create from an existing contact, the source stays copied from that contact.

Referenced Contacts

You can link a new contact to an existing one (e.g., a spouse). When you do, both contacts are assigned to the same agent.

🔄
From a Customer Follow-Up

On a follow-up's edit popup, Convert to Contact opens this same Create Contact form with name, phone, email, and comments filled in. If that phone already exists, you are taken to the existing contact instead. Either way the follow-up is marked inactive and leaves the follow-up list.

🗃 Contact Financial Profile (Statement of Position)

The contact detail page captures a complete financial picture across these sections:

SectionWhat It Contains
👤 Personal DetailsName, DOB, gender, marital status, number of kids and each child's age (up to 5), identification documents (Driver's License, Passport, Photo ID, Proof of Age Card)
🏠 Residential HistoryEvery address lived at, duration, whether owned/rented/boarded
💼 Employment detailsUp to 3 current jobs, each with employment type, pay frequency (weekly / fortnightly / monthly / annually), and salary as entered. One rental income figure for the contact
💼 Employment HistoryJobs held over time: employer, phone, occupation, start and finish dates. Only one job can be marked as current
💰 AssetsReal estate, vehicles, savings, superannuation, shares, others with values

💵 ExpensesRent, utilities, insurance, childcare, other recurring costs
🛡 InsuranceLife, income protection, health policies with premiums

🔎 Contact List Features

  • Search Anything Type any keyword to instantly filter the full contact list
  • Column Picker Choose which columns to show or hide (your choice is remembered)
  • Privacy Protection Online-channel contacts' phone and email are hidden from agents who don't own them
  • Edit contact information From the contact detail header, edit name (and email when you are allowed to see it), plus street address, suburb, state, and postcode. Address fields are optional; leaving one blank keeps the value already saved
  • Bulk Upload Import many contacts at once from a CSV file, with duplicate checking
  • Export to Excel Download or email the list (Manager/Admin only). Colour-coded by source
  • Delete Contact Manager/Admin only. Removes the contact and deactivates all related deals and reminders

Deals (Loan Pipeline)

A Deal is a specific loan opportunity for a customer. In this example, one customer can have several deals at once, up to five open at a time, each for a different loan type.

📈 The Deal Board (Kanban View)

Deals are displayed as cards on a visual board, organized into columns:

Deal Board Columns
🔷 Open PipelineActive deals progressing through stages
➔
✅ Closed WonLoan settled!
❌ Closed LostDid not proceed

Each deal card shows: Customer name, deal number, loan amount (AUD), current stage, deal age, source colour, temperature (Hot/Warm/Cold), agent, and current assignee.

🛠 What You Can Do With Deals

▶ Moving Deals

  • Drag cards between columns (in this example: Lead, Manager, Admin)
  • Use Next/Previous stage buttons
  • Pick any stage manually

✎ Editing Deals

  • Change assignee
  • Change deal temperature (Hot/Warm/Cold)
  • Change loan type (resets to start)
  • Edit requested loan amount

📩 Communications

  • Send ad-hoc email to customer
  • Send Privacy Consent for signing
  • Send SOP document for signing

📁 Deal Details

  • Lender applications tab
  • Comments thread
  • File attachments, including download all as one package
  • Full audit log
  • Email history
⚠
Assignee-only, in this example

Picture a firm where only the person currently assigned to a deal can change its stage, add applications, or edit the requested amount. Anyone else is asked to leave that step to the assignee.

📁
Files stay with the deal

Upload supporting documents on the deal, then download them one by one or all together as a single package. In this example, files on loans that closed some time ago are packed up each month and stored in Google Drive, so the office copy does not grow forever.

🔏 Stage Change Process

Moving a deal to the next stage may involve several checks, depending on how the stage is set up:

1
Assignee Check

In this example, only the current assignee moves the deal. Others are asked to wait.

2
Stage Checklist

Some stages ask for a short list first, such as documents uploaded or an application submitted. In this example, each item is confirmed before the deal moves.

3
Reason Selection

Certain stages (like "Not Eligible" or "Withdrawn") require you to pick a reason from a list.

4
Customer Communication

Some stages require you to send the customer an email or message. You can also skip this (it gets logged).

5
Settlement Details (Closed Won only)

In this example, closing a deal as won asks for the lender, disbursed amount, closure date, and approval details.

🚫
Moving backward

In this example, a move back to an earlier stage asks for a short reason. That note stays in the history.

🔒
Final stages stay final, in this example

Picture Closed Won, Closed Lost, and Deferred as locked once chosen. A different firm could allow a reopen. This sample does not.

Application Exit Paths (Can Happen at Any Point)

At each step, the system records the relevant amount: amount requested, amount offered, amount approved, and amount settled. Dates are captured at each transition. In this example, a final status (Settled, Declined, Withdrawn, Customer Denied, or Not Eligible) locks that application.

📝 Manual Follow-Ups (Customer Follow-Ups)

You can also create follow-ups that aren't tied to a specific deal, like callback requests or general check-ins. These live on the Customer Follow Ups list (Create New → Follow Up, or the Follow Ups menu).

Section 10

Sales & Earnings

✅ Sales

When a deal is closed as won and the lender has disbursed the loan, it becomes a Sale. The Sales page shows a searchable, sortable table with:

  • Customer Name and Agent Name who managed the deal
  • Team Lead supervising the agent
  • Loan Type and Lender
  • Requested Amount vs Disbursed Amount (both in AUD)
  • Closure Date

In this example, Leads, Managers, and Admins see this page, and Agents do not. Managers and Admins can export to Excel.

💲 Earnings (Commission Tracking)

A separate, password-protected area for tracking commissions on each sale. You can record the commission amount, date, and notes per sale, and view period summaries. This is a private area with its own access control.

🚫 When Transfers Are Blocked

Requests sit on a Requests list until an approver decides. In this sample, the request is refused in these situations:

SituationResult
Customer has open (active) dealsBlocked - Wait for current deals to close
Customer created less than 91 days agoBlocked - Reach out to your manager
Last deal closed within 90 daysBlocked - 90-day cooling-off period
Customer transferred within last 90 daysBlocked - Transfer cooldown active
Already a pending request for this customerBlocked - One request at a time
No blocks applyAllowed - Proceed with request

A sample incentive choice

In this example, a transferred customer does not count toward the new agent's leads bonus. Only customers that agent originally created count. An approver can also be allowed to waive the cooling-off window when the business needs the move sooner.

📝 Email Templates

Templates are ready-made messages that fill in customer and deal details, such as name, loan type, sender, interest rate, term, and lender. Email sends from the connected business mailbox. SMS and WhatsApp wording can be stored beside those email templates.

Every email (sent, failed, or skipped) is logged for audit purposes.

📄 Privacy Consent

  • Required before sharing info with lenders
  • Can include lender-specific documents
  • Customer must accept consent checkbox

📋 Statement of Position

  • Full financial profile summary
  • Generated from contact details in the system
  • Up to 3 current jobs print separately (type and salary)

Section 17

Sample Operating Rules

These are assumed rules for the imaginary brokerage in this guide. They show the kind of guardrails Calicon CRM can hold. The numbers are examples, not a fixed package.

1
One Owner Per Customer

In this example, every customer belongs to one agent. A change goes through an approved transfer.

2
Up to 5 Open Deals

In this example, a customer can have at most five active deals at a time. Close one before opening another.

3
Assignee-Only Actions

In this example, only the person assigned to a deal changes its stage, adds applications, or edits amounts.

4
Backward Moves Need a Reason

In this example, moving a deal to an earlier stage asks for a written explanation.

5
Closed Deals Stay Closed

In this example, Closed Won, Lost, and Deferred are not reopened.

6
90-Day Transfer Cooldown

In this example, after a transfer or a closure, the customer stays put for 90 days.

7
91-Day New Contact Protection

In this example, a new contact cannot be claimed by another agent until they are 91 days old.

8
Transfers Sit Outside the Leads Bonus

In this example, only customers you originally created count toward the leads bonus.

9
Stage Checklists

In this example, the checks you configured are completed, or skipped with a note, before the deal moves on.

10
Everything Is Audited

Every change, email, login, approval, and export is permanently recorded in the audit log.
