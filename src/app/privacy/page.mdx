# Privacy

**Effective date:** 15 May 2026

This notice describes how Calicon ("we", "us") handles personal information
collected through this website, including enquiry forms.

## What we collect

When you submit a form, we may collect your name, email address, company name,
phone or WhatsApp number, and any details you choose to provide about your
project or situation.

If you arrive from a campaign link, we may also store non-sensitive source
details from the URL (for example `utm_source`) in your browser session so we
can respond with relevant context. Those details are included with your enquiry
if you submit the form. They are not used to build advertising profiles.

## How we use it

We use this information to respond to your enquiry, understand what you need,
and continue the conversation. We do not sell personal information. We do not
run aggressive automated sales sequences from this site.

## Retention

We retain enquiry data only as long as needed for those purposes or as required
by law. You may request access or deletion of your personal information by
contacting us using the details you already have.

## Hosting and processors

This site may be hosted on infrastructure providers (for example Vercel) and use
email delivery services when you connect them. Those providers process data as
processors under our instructions.

## Cookies and analytics

Analytics is optional and off unless a measurement ID is configured. When it is
on, we use it to understand which pages help qualified conversations — not as a
vanity-traffic scoreboard. We honor Do Not Track in supporting browsers: if it
is set, we do not load third-party analytics. We do not use invasive tracking,
heatmaps, session recording, marketing popups, or advertising profiles.

Analytics events describe pages and actions (for example, that someone opened
contact or chose a path). We do not send form contents, names, email addresses,
phone numbers, project descriptions, or other enquiry text to analytics. Query
strings are not included in analytics page paths.

## Contact

For privacy questions, reach out through the contact channel listed on this site.
