# Privacy

**Effective date:** 15 May 2026

This notice describes how Calicap ("we", "us") handles personal information
collected through this website, including enquiry forms.

## What we collect

When you submit a form, we may collect your name, email address, company name,
budget range, and any details you choose to provide about your project.

## How we use it

We use this information to respond to your enquiry, assess fit for services, and
communicate about next steps. We do not sell personal information.

## Retention

We retain enquiry data only as long as needed for those purposes or as required
by law. You may request access or deletion of your personal information by
contacting us using the details you already have.

## Hosting and processors

This site may be hosted on infrastructure providers (for example Vercel) and use
email delivery services when you connect them. Those providers process data as
processors under our instructions.

## Cookies and analytics

If we enable analytics or marketing tags, we will align deployment with applicable
law and, where required, your consent choices.

## Contact

For privacy questions, reach out through the contact channel listed on this site.
