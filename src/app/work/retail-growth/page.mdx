# Retail brand lift

**Engagement:** website rebuild, technical SEO, editorial system  
**Timeline:** 14 weeks  
**Role:** strategy, design direction, build leadership

---

## Challenge

A multi-location retail brand had outgrown a template stack. Product lines were strong,
but the site hid expertise: slow on mobile, thin on structured data, and editorial
updates required developer intervention for routine launches.

## Approach

We reframed the top-level IA around purchase intents—not internal org charts—and
paired it with a minimal design system that could flex for campaigns without
re-theming. Technical work prioritised Core Web Vitals, crawl budget, and clean
schema for locations and products.

## Outcomes

- **Faster perceived performance** on key templates after re-architecting LCP assets  
- **Clearer enquiry path** with a single primary CTA per section  
- **Publishing cadence** aligned to seasons—no deploy for routine edits
